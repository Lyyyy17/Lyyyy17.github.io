Universal Microwave Technology, Inc. (3491.TW)

Equity Research & Stock Pitch · Valuation · Fundamental Analysis

BUY with a target price of NT$405 — 41.2% upside — on the thesis that UMT is the purest Taiwan-listed play on LEO satellite component demand, backed by a bottom-up revenue model of its order book from SpaceX and Amazon Kuiper.

Investment Question

UMT is Taiwan's only dedicated designer-manufacturer of microwave/mmWave passive components. With the LEO satellite build-out accelerating (51,000 satellites planned for deployment by 2030), the question was whether the growth was already priced in — and how much of the order-book momentum was durable versus one-off.

Method

  • Bottom-up revenue model by product line: LEO components modeled from disclosed order books and the launch schedules of SpaceX (~30% of segment) and Amazon Kuiper (~60%, incl. a 4-year exclusive supply contract); 4G/5G backhaul at 12.9% CAGR on North-American infrastructure spending; RF antennas and network engineering from subsidiary guidance.
  • Margin & cost structure: gross margin by product mix, opex as 4-quarter moving average, explicit assumptions for non-operating items and a 20% tax rate.
  • Valuation: P/E band analysis ("river chart") against the company's historical range — 25× applied to 2025F EPS of NT$16.21.
  • Risk assessment: launch-schedule slippage (only SpaceX was >50% complete on announced constellations) and Starship test outcomes as the key swing factor for launch costs.

Key Findings

NT$405 · +41.2%

Target price at 25× 2025F EPS 16.21 vs NT$287 close (as of 2024/05/31)

NT$660M backlog

LEO orders on hand for 2Q–4Q24 — already exceeding the prior three years' total shipments (NT$540M)

5-year cycle

Satellite payload replacement period — recurring demand, not one-off; capacity +40% by 2025 to absorb it

  • Payload components are the core driver: payload share of LEO product mix rose from 60% to 70–80% after new orders from the two major US customers; satellite payloads are replaced roughly every 5 years, turning launches into a recurring revenue stream.
  • Customer concentration is a feature and a risk: Kuiper's 2025 orders were guided at ~3× the 2024 level with exclusive supply on 8 contracted component types (10+ actually shipping), likely making it UMT's largest customer.
  • Non-China supply positioning (Taiwan + Southeast Asia fabs) aligns with US operators' de-China sourcing policies.

Outcome

Recommendation: accumulate on dips, TP NT$405. The pitch integrated industry sizing from my LEO satellite industry report with company-level modeling — the two projects together demonstrate a full research workflow from macro thesis to single-name valuation.
