EverPresent 禮悟 — Gift-Giving Platform App

UX Research & Product Design · Team Project · Interviews, Contextual Inquiry, Wireframing, Hi-Fi Prototyping

An invisible wall sits between digital natives and older generations — we care, but the feelings drift apart in silence. EverPresent turns gift-giving into an icebreaker: an AI companion that translates family memories and life stories into meaningful, purchasable gifts. I worked across two full research-and-iteration cycles, from benchmarking existing gifting platforms to a working interactive demo.

Research — Phase 1: Where Existing Platforms Fail

  • Method: scenario-based user interviews and contextual inquiry on two existing gifting products (an AI gift recommender and a gifting e-commerce site), using realistic tasks like "find a gift that evokes Grandpa's fishing-village memories, under NT$6,000."
  • Users start with the person, not the product — they think about who Grandpa is and what he remembers, while platforms force stories to be compressed into fragmented keywords.
  • Hard constraints can't be wrong: a single miss on age, budget, or availability destroys trust instantly.
  • No "AI feel": when the system doesn't ask follow-up questions or explain its reasoning, users perceive it as ordinary search — and when results fail, there's no recovery path beyond retyping keywords.

Personas

Three personas anchored the design space: a 27-year-old engineer who wants an "algorithmic" tool to translate memories into a precise gift; a 20-year-old student who wants gifts carrying shared memories but drowns in options; and a 65-year-old retiree with deep stories to tell but no patience for complicated tech. The common thread — everyone struggles to turn abstract memories into tangible gifts.

Design Iteration — Phase 2: Testing Our Own Prototype

  • From sketch to hi-fi: wireframes were usability-tested, and the second interview round tested the hi-fi version against a real gifting task.
  • Cold-start fix: users froze at "input your story," so we added suggested chips ("most memorable trip," "things overcome together") and AI follow-up questions that deconstruct needs step by step.
  • Progress and control: a visible collection checklist (✓ shared memories ✓ their interests ▢ recent needs) with autosave, and the AI's interpretation externalized ("I understand your needs as…") so users can confirm or correct it before recommendations.
  • Language matters: e-commerce terms like "Complete Purchase" broke the emotional frame — renamed flows to "Create Gift Solution" and "Generate Gift Card," and recommendations stay inside the conversation so users can adjust in natural language ("not handmade types").

Core Features

  • Memories archive: the home screen is a living family archive — not a product catalog — where members add and share memories.
  • AI gift chatbot: conversational recommendation grounded in the recipient's memories, with photo / audio / video attachments unified into the input box.
  • Gift suggestions: Top 6 ranked by memory overlap and emotional value, with real product info, prices, and order tracking.
  • Family-tree profile: gift history and relationships build into a family tree over time, making each future gift better informed.
